                        CASE CLOSED - RESOLUTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

After careful analysis of all evidence, the identity of the killer
is revealed:

                    *** LADY VICTORIA ***

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE FULL STORY:
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Five years of embezzlement had left Lady Victoria's finances in
ruins. Her daughter's medical bills were astronomical. She was
facing financial ruin.

When she discovered Pemberton's theft through Dr. Stone's report,
something snapped. This man had stolen her future, her daughter's
future, everything.

THE PLAN:
  1. She visited the garden shed and stole the cyanide compound
  2. She crushed almonds to mask the taste
  3. She instructed the kitchen staff to prepare wine for Pemberton
  4. SHE, HERSELF, brought the poisoned wine to his study
  5. She left her handkerchief as unintentional evidence

THE MISDIRECTION:
  • The locked room made it seem impossible
  • The torn jacket fragment in the library (planted by her)
  • The fake evidence pointing to Roland Davies
  • The complex financial scheme distracted investigators

THE UNRAVELING:
  • Mrs. Hayes remembered that Lady Victoria herself brought the tray
  • The handkerchief with cyanide traces in the garden shed
  • Her unusual interest in the pesticide storage
  • Her knowledge of Pemberton's schedule
  • The library mystery - SHE put James' jacket there as a frame!

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHY OTHERS WEREN'T GUILTY:

JAMES FLETCHER:
  • Present in the library (investigating the embezzlement)
  • Not complicit in the murder
  • His torn jacket was taken and used as false evidence

ROLAND DAVIES:
  • Innocent despite owning the cyanide
  • Was set up with the wet jacket and cyanide bottle
  • Left early to avoid the scene (he sensed danger)
  • The muddy footprints were from earlier in the day

DR. MARGARET STONE:
  • Discovered the crime, reported it
  • Had medical knowledge but wrong motive
  • Was unaware of Lady Victoria's desperation
  • Her only crime was remaining silent about suspicions

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FINAL CONFESSION:
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When confronted with the evidence, Lady Victoria broke down:

"He ruined my life. My daughter is sick and I cannot afford her
treatment. That money was OURS. He was a thief.

When I learned he had stolen a quarter million pounds, I was
overcome. I thought of my failure as a mother, my inability to
save my daughter.

In that moment, I knew what had to be done. I did it for her.
For my daughter. I have no regrets."
