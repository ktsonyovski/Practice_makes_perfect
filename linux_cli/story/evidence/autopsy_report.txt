                        AUTOPSY REPORT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

VICTIM: Mr. Edmund Pemberton, Age 47
TIME OF DEATH: Approximately 12:00 AM (midnight)
CAUSE OF DEATH: Acute cyanide poisoning

DETAILS:
The victim shows classic signs of cyanide poisoning:
  • Facial flushing and bright red discoloration
  • Smell of bitter almonds on the breath
  • Foaming at the mouth
  • Dilated pupils

TOXICOLOGY: 
  • Cyanide concentration in blood: 4.2 mg/L (lethal dose)
  • Found in stomach contents: wine and crushed almonds
  • Time of ingestion: Approximately 11:50 PM
  • The poison was mixed into the wine

STOMACH ANALYSIS:
Contents include: Cheese, bread, wine, and crushed almonds mixed
with cyanide compound. The almond pieces suggest an attempt to mask
the bitter taste of the poison.

CONCLUSION: Poisoning was deliberate and premeditated.
