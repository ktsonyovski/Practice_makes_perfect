                    VICTIM - BACKGROUND PROFILE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

NAME: Edmund Pemberton
AGE: 47
PROFESSION: Butler and Estate Manager
EMPLOYED: 25 years at Midnight Manor
SALARY: £40,000/year + room and board

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PERSONAL HISTORY:
  • Started as junior servant in 1998
  • Promoted to butler in 2005 due to excellent performance
  • Managed estate finances with increasing responsibility
  • Known as professional, reliable, and trustworthy

REPUTATION:
  • Highly regarded by Lady Victoria (she trusted him completely)
  • Respected by staff for his fairness
  • Known in town as a man of integrity
  • No criminal record or history of problems

LIFESTYLE:
  • Quiet, unassuming personal life
  • Modest living quarters in the manor
  • No known vices or indulgences
  
THE FALL: Somewhere in his fifth year as manager, Pemberton began
siphoning money. No one knows why. Perhaps financial pressure?
Perhaps simple greed? The cause died with him.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

His death ends the investigation as much as it begins it.
His crimes died with him, but so did his explanations.
