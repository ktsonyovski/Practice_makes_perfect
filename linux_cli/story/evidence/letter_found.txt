                    THE TORN LETTER - FULL VERSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This is the complete letter found partially burned in the fireplace.
Recovered through careful analysis of ash patterns and ink residue.

FULL TEXT:
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

"Dear Pemberton,

I have discovered your secret about the embezzlement. For five years,
you have been siphoning £50,000 annually from Lady Victoria's accounts.

I confronted the others about this. We agreed it must end tonight.

The three of us discussed the matter, and we decided James would 
handle the financial records. But things went wrong. When I told 
you I knew, you became aggressive. You threatened to destroy us all.

In the struggle, James lost his jacket button. The gardener became
nervous. Someone said they would 'take care of it.' But I never 
expected them to actually go through with poison.

I confess my involvement in the scheme to hide the embezzlement, but
I swear I did not poison you.

Your former friend,
M.P."
