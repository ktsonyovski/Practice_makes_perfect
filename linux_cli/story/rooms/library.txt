Floor-to-ceiling bookshelves line the mahogany-paneled walls.
A crystal chandelier casts dancing shadows. The room smells of
old paper and leather.

What you notice:

  • Several medical books are scattered on a side table
  • A journal dated from last month contains notes about Pemberton's

    was dragged

CLUE: A torn piece of fabric catches your eye under the bookshelf.
It's midnight blue, expensive looking. You recognize this fabric - 
it matches the jacket James Fletcher was wearing!
