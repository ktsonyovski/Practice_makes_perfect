The study is dimly lit by a dying fire. Mr. Pemberton lies slumped
in his leather chair behind the mahogany desk. An empty wine glass
sits beside him. A faint smell of bitter almonds lingers in the air.

On the desk:
  • A half-written letter (unfinished)
  • A pocket watch stopped at 12:00
  • A leather journal with initials "M.P."
  • Three photographs

OBSERVATIONS:

  • A back door to the garden is slightly ajar
  • In the fireplace: burnt papers and a single silver button
  
CLUE: The letter on the desk reads:
"Dear Pemberton, I have discovered your secret about the—"
The rest is torn away.
